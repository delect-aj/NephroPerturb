6 Agent Skills & Execution Logic

Modular ReAct Playbooks
Every research query is routed deterministically to one focused Skill with scoped tools, safety reflection rules, and a fixed 4-section synthesis format.

How Auto Skill Routes Your Query

Zero-LLM rule router runs in <1 ms before any model call
1Manual Skill Selection
If you click a specific Skill pill in the console, Auto routing is skipped and that Skill runs directly.
2Compare & Safety Rescue
Keywords like compare/vs trigger Compare Drugs; nephrotoxic/safer analogs/T-DIKI/DIRIL trigger Safety & Rescue.
3Literature & Session Follow-ups
Pure PubMed/clinical trial queries (or follow-ups referencing #1, #2, or prior candidates) route to Literature & Trials.
4Deterministic SOP-1 to SOP-6 & Guardrail
Remaining queries match Up/Down genes, cohorts (DKD/LN/IgAN/AKI), pathways, target knockdowns, or SMILES scaffolds; non-kidney topics are stopped safely.

Skill 1 · Disease -> Evidencedisease-repurposingMax 6 steps

Disease-to-Evidence Repurposing

When selected: Kidney cohorts (DKD, LN, IgAN, AKI), Up/Down gene signatures, or Pathway/TF screens.
1
Screen Drug Candidatesrun_cohort_casescore_gene_signaturequery_pathway_tf
Ranks 21,299 compounds by transcriptional disease-reversal and inspects Top-3 hits for High T-DIKI or FDA DIRIL+ toxicity flags.
2
Auto-Rescue Toxic Top Hitsre-screen (Low T-DIKI)find_safe_analogs
Runs automatically only if a Top-3 lead is flagged toxic, switching to approved Low T-DIKI drugs or safer structural analogs.
3
Deep-Profile #1 Safe Leadprofile_compound
Fetches the #1 safe compound by exact pert_id to examine its targets, MoA, PROGENy pathways, 9 renal modules, and K-PRL genes.
4
Verify Literature & Trialssearch_literaturesearch_clinical_trials
Queries Europe PMC / PubMed (Title/Abstract for both efficacy and nephrotoxicity) plus ClinicalTrials.gov for the Top 1-2 leads, then writes the verified report.
Fixed Report Sections: 1. Executive Synthesis & Prioritized Candidates -> 2. Mechanistic & NephroSafe Rationale -> 3. Integrated Literature & Clinical Review -> 4. Translational Recommendation & Next Steps
Allowed Tools: run_cohort_case, score_gene_signature, query_pathway_tf, profile_compound, find_safe_analogs, search_literature, search_clinical_trials
